C. Aortic regurgitation

An early diastolic decrescendo murmur at the left upper sternal border, best heard sitting forward in end-expiration, is classic for aortic regurgitation. Chronic volume overload dilates and thickens the left ventricle, explaining the cardiomegaly and LVH. Aortic stenosis is a systolic ejection murmur, mitral stenosis is a mid-diastolic rumble at the apex, and mitral regurgitation is holosystolic at the apex.

Key point Early diastolic murmur at the left upper sternal border, louder sitting forward = aortic regurgitation.
